An agent is ready to deploy a release. It sends May I? the environment, release digest, and current version it expects to replace. A person sees that exact request and decides.

If the request is approved, May I? signs a short-lived receipt tied to those details. Change the target or the release and the receipt will not verify.

## A small, deliberate boundary

May I? does not try to decide whether an action is wise. It records what the agent wants to do, gives the right person a clear review, and produces proof of their answer.

That makes it useful for deployments, destructive admin work, money movement, and any other action where a vague "looks good" is not enough.
